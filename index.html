2-PLAYER: Press C for split-screen co-op. With one controller, P1 plays on keyboard + mouse and P2 takes the controller. With two controllers, each player gets one. Plug in a second controller and co-op starts on its own.
THE COASTER: Underground — take the shaft by the gold beacon. Lift hill, big drop, a full vertical loop that turns you upside down, hairpin turns, a corkscrew, and a jump where the rails stop and the cart flies through the air (yellow markers show takeoff and landing). There are two carts: in co-op both players ride at once, P1 with R, P2 with X.
NUKE & TNT x100: Both are in the hotbar (slots 6 and 7). TNT x100 drops a 5×4×5 block of 100 real TNT — light any one and they chain-detonate one after another. The NUKE arms with a 3-second countdown and clears 1000× the volume of a single TNT. Neither one can hurt you — the nuke is safe to stand near and watch.
THE CAVE DRAGON: Look for the huge black ring of pillars with gold spikes on the surface — it is impossible to miss. Climb down the shaft into the arena and the dragon wakes up. It circles, dives at you and breathes purple fire; shoot the head for big damage. Rockets and the sword work too. Kill it for a pile of gold and diamond.
Weapons: Sword (melee), Bow (arc arrows), Rifle (fast bullets), Rocket Launcher (blows craters in the world). Zombies spawn in the dark and burn up at sunrise. The roller coaster is underground — find the gold beacon and climb down the shaft. Press H to close.
