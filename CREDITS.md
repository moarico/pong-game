# Credits

Stormdrop's code is original. Its characters, their animation, the cars and the street props come from
artists who released their work into the public domain (CC0). Thank you!

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Character Pack: Adventurers (animation set) | [Kay Lousberg / KayKit](https://www.kaylousberg.com) | CC0 1.0 | Idle, walk, run, strafe, jump, aim, reload, swing, sit, cheer and more, retargeted onto the Kenney skeleton in `assets/characters/character.glb` |
| Car Kit (parked and drivable cars) | [Kenney](https://kenney.nl), via the [pmndrs market](https://github.com/pmndrs/market-assets) | CC0 1.0 | `assets/models/car-*.glb` |
| City Builder Bits (street lamps, benches, hydrants, dumpsters, boxes) | [Kay Lousberg / KayKit](https://www.kaylousberg.com) | CC0 1.0 | `assets/models/city-*.glb` |
| three.js r186 and its glTF/HDR loaders and skeleton utilities | three.js authors | MIT | Rendering, `vendor/` |

The two legendary outfits (Nightfall, Sun Marshal) are recolors of Kenney skins made in code at load time.
